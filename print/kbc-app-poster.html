Keep it one tap away

Save it to your home screen and it gets its own icon, like any other app.

iPhone & iPad — in Safari

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share — the square with the arrow coming out of it.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Safari only. Chrome on an iPhone has no Add to Home Screen — if you scanned with a different browser, paste the link into Safari first.

Android — in Chrome

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen — or Install app, if it offers that.
  4. Tap Install.

Chrome may offer to install it on its own — a bar across the bottom of the screen. Taking that does the same thing.
