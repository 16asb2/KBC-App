Purchases
Import Purchases from CSV

Paste the sheet or choose a .csv file. The first row must be headers — column order doesn't matter, and unrecognised columns are ignored. Member Name, Date and Purchase are required.

These are records of what was bought and when. They appear in the purchase history and on receipts; they do not give anyone a pass or add punches to a profile — set a member's current pass on the Members tab. Re-importing the same file overwrites the same entries rather than adding duplicates, because each row's id is built from the member, the moment and the purchase.

Recognised columns: Member Name · Email · Date · Purchase · Amount · Notes

Purchase accepts the same names as the member import: Annual (or 12 months, Student Annual) · 8-month · 4-month · 1-month · Punch Pass · Drop-In (or Day Pass). Amount is optional and free-form ($160, 160) — left out, the current price list is used.

Member Directory
Import Members from CSV

Paste the sheet or choose a .csv file. The first row must be headers — column order doesn't matter, and unrecognised columns are ignored. Email is required: it is what links a member to their account when they first sign in. Rows missing anything else are still imported and flagged, and the member is asked to fill the gaps the first time they open the app.

Re-importing updates. A row whose email already belongs to a member edits that member rather than adding a second one. Only the columns your file carries are touched — a blank cell leaves what is stored alone, and waivers, punch history and the member's linked account are never affected. The preview lists exactly what each row will change before anything is written.

Recognised columns: Legal Name · Preferred Name · Email · Phone · Emergency Contact · Relationship · Emergency Phone · Access Pass · Confirmed · Access Pass Start · Expiry · Member Since · Punches · Supervisor · Admin

Access Pass is the pass the member holds — it is written to the membershipAccessPass field, and the column is named to match. It accepts: Annual (or 12 months, Student Annual) · 8-month · 4-month · 1-month · Punch Pass (10 punches unless a Punches column says otherwise) · Drop-In · None. A dated pass runs from Access Pass Start — or Member Since, or today — and the end date is worked out from its length unless the file gives one; a row with dates and no pass column gets the pass those dates describe. Punch passes and drop-ins grant no period, so they carry no dates.

Confirmed is the separate question of whether an admin has confirmed the payment — Yes/No, or Pending for not yet. Left out, a pass is taken as confirmed. There is no Status column any more: it used to mean both of these at once, which is why a sheet saying "Annual pass" under it imported as nothing at all.
